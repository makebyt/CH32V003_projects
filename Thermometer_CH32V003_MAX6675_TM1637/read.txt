++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++

CH32V003J4M6 (24 MHz)
For MAX6675 sensor

Temperature is displayed in whole degrees Celsius,
without decimal places.

Connection
SO - PA1[1]
SCK - PA2[3]
CS - PC4[7]

TM1637 Temperature Pin
DIO - PC2[6]
CLK - PC1[5]

When the Li-ion battery is low, the display shows 'L'

SPI polling rate is approximately 500 kHz
The entire polling process takes approximately 35 µS
